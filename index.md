This blog is about building stuff for the joy of it, creating out of passion and growing my skillset for my own enjoyment and fulfillment without the expectation of popularity or success, just slow, steady and shared work, hence, the name of the blog: **quiet-wins**.

For a long time, I waited for the _perfect opportunity_ to start any of the countless ideas in my head. It never arrived, and I learned the hard way:

> Perfect is the enemy of good

That said, check the [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) section for finished builds—write-ups detailing the steps I took to ship them.

Thanks for reading—hope you enjoy! :)

**Honestly, f\*ck FOMO.**
